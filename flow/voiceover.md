# Voiceover scripts: bwm-01, bwm-02, bwm-03

Generate with a generic AI voice (not a clone of Manjot). Suggested: a young Indian male voice,
confident and conversational, slightly fast. In ElevenLabs use the Multilingual v2 model, which reads
romanized Hinglish well.

- One MP3 per reel, named `bwm-01-vo.mp3`, `bwm-02-vo.mp3`, `bwm-03-vo.mp3`.
- Paste each block as one take. The blank lines are pauses; keep them.
- Aim for 28-31 s per reel. The edit retimes captions and scenes to the audio, so exact timing
  doesn't matter.
- No music in the file; music gets added in Instagram.

## bwm-01

```
Client bola: mujhe ek AI employee chahiye.

Salary nahi. Chai nahi. Leave nahi.

Sach bataun? AI employee nahi hota. AI workflow hota hai. Ek kaam, poora automate.

Us client ke liye humne support ke 10 sawaal automate kiye. Team wahi, kaam aadha.

Tumhare office mein kaunsa kaam roz repeat hota hai?

Comment WORKFLOW, and I'll send you the checklist we use to pick what to automate.
```
