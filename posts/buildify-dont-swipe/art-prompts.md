# Illustration prompts for the Buildify "Don't swipe" carousel

Generate in Google Flow (image mode) or any image model. Settings: 4:5 portrait, best quality.
Save as `art/s1.png` ... `art/s4.png`. A plain white background is fine; a transparent PNG is better.
Use the same style line on every image so the four slides match.

STYLE (append to every prompt):
Hand-drawn ink illustration with loose watercolour fill, fine pen hatching, graphic-novel style,
warm burnt-orange and tan clothing as the only strong colour, muted greys and browns elsewhere,
full body, plain pure white background, no text, no logos, small dusty ground patch under the feet.
