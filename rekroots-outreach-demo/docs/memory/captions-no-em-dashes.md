Never use em dashes (—) in post captions written for the content calendars or social
posts. Use a period, comma, colon, or a line break instead.

**Why:** em dashes read as AI-written and break the brand voice. The rule is already in
the brand docs (`DESIGN.md` and `DESIGN-PRISM.md` both say "no buzzwords, no em dashes"
under Voice), but it applies to captions too, not just on-screen reel copy. Flagged after
a Rekroots calendar caption shipped with em dashes in its numbered lines.

**How to apply:** when writing any `Caption` field in the Notion content calendars, or any
reel/carousel copy, scan for `—` before saving. Rewrite `1️⃣ Screening — AI reads every CV`
as `1️⃣ Screening. AI reads every CV`. Applies to [[content-strategy-playbook]] output and
anything built through [[reel-pipeline]].
