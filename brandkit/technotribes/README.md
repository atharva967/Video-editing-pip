# TechnoTribes

TechnoTribes is an India-first enterprise AI implementation company: production AI workflows, AI agents and automation for enterprise teams, shipped in 30 to 90 days. This brand kit is taken from the live site, technotribes.ai, as it stood on 19 November 2026.

## Logo

The logo is a **circuit "T"**: a capital T drawn from circuit traces, in orange (`orange`) shading into deep red, set beside the wordmark **TECHNOTRIBES** in uppercase.

- **Official file:** `https://www.technotribes.ai/logo.svg` (vector). It is not stored in this kit yet; add it to the Logos group when you have the file.
- **Lockup:** the mark at 30px, then a 10px gap, then the wordmark in Syne semibold, 13px, uppercase, `0.25em` tracking, in white on `surface`.
- **Never** redraw, recolour or stretch the mark. Give it clear space of at least its own height.

## Voice

Direct, confident, enterprise. Lead with the outcome and the timeline, not the technology.

- Headlines are short claims: "Enterprise AI", "Workflows shipped in 30-90 days".
- Supporting copy names real teams and real work: "sales, hiring, support, operations, and internal knowledge systems".
- Calls to action are specific: "Book AI strategy call", "View enterprise sprint".
- Write "TechnoTribes" in running text; the wordmark itself is uppercase.

## Colour

TechnoTribes is a **dark-only** brand: near-black `surface` (#0C0C0C) with light `ink` text, one brand colour and one action colour.

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `surface` | #0C0C0C | Every background |
| `ink` | #E8E8E8 | Headlines and body |
| `ink-muted` | #999999 | Supporting copy |
| `orange` | #DD6710 | Brand: the logo mark and brand highlights |
| `red` | #EF4444 | Action: links, hovers, primary button text |
| `cta-fill` / `line-maroon` | #250F0F / #5F1616 | Primary button fill and border |
| `hairline` | #242424 | Secondary button borders, dividers |

Rules:
- Orange is the brand, red is the action. Don't use orange for links or buttons, or red for the logo.
- Keep colour scarce: most of a page is `surface` and `ink`, with a red accent where someone should click.
- `ink-subtle` (#777777) is only 4.3:1 on `surface`: fine for 13px nav links, never for paragraphs.

## Typography

Three Google Fonts families:

- **Syne** (`display`): hero headlines, section headings and the wordmark. Big, tight and wide.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** (`sans`): body copy, navigation, buttons and eyebrows.
- **JetBrains Mono** (`mono`): small technical labels and numbers.

The signature move is contrast of scale: a huge Syne headline (153.6px on desktop) over a small, widely tracked uppercase line in Plus Jakarta Sans. Hero and section sizes come from the site's styles; eyebrow and body sizes were measured from a 1920px-wide screenshot.

## Shape and layout

- **Buttons are pills** (`radius-pill`); everything else is square (`radius-none`).
- Primary button: `cta-fill` with a `line-maroon` border and `red` uppercase label. Secondary button: transparent with a `hairline` border and `ink-muted` label.
- An 8px spacing grid (`space-1`). Side gutters grow with the screen: 20px on phones, `space-4` on tablets, `space-8` and wider on desktop, inside a 1600px maximum width.
- A 1px `red-deep` progress line runs under the fixed header as the page scrolls.

## Iconography

There is no icon set on the site beyond the logo mark. Use thin, single-weight line icons in `ink-muted`, turning `red` on hover, if a page needs them.
