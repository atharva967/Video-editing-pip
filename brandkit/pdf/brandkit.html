01 · Logo

The circuit T

The logo is a capital T drawn from circuit traces, in orange shading into deep red, set beside the wordmark TECHNOTRIBES. It stands for what we do: engineering that turns AI into working systems.

TECHNOTRIBES
  • Mark at 30px, 10px gap, then the wordmark.

02 · Voice

Direct. Confident. Enterprise.

Lead with the outcome and the timeline, not the technology. Name real teams and real work.

We say

  • "Enterprise AI. Workflows shipped in 30–90 days."
  • "From AI experiments to production workflows across sales, hiring, support and operations."
  • "Book AI strategy call"

We avoid

  • Buzzwords: "revolutionary", "next-gen", "synergy".
  • Vague promises with no timeline or outcome.
  • Generic CTAs like "Learn more" or "Click here".

Write TechnoTribes in running text. The wordmark itself is uppercase.
